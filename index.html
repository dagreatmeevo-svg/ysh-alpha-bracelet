Tying chart ✏️ Painting ON

→ odd rows: tie left to right · forward knots ← even rows: tie right to left · backward knots
Row 1 is the start of the bracelet. Tap a row on the chart or in the list to jump to it. Each row lists its colours in the order you tie them, following the arrow. The letters lie on their side in the chart and read upright on the finished bracelet.

Change knots

Painting stays locked so a wrong tap never changes a colour. Unlock it to paint: pick a colour, then tap knots on the chart. Lock it again when you're done. Undo takes back the last knot.
